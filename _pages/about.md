I am a postdoctoral researcher at [NTT Social Informatics Laboratories](https://www.rd.ntt/e/sil/) in Tokyo,
working on post-quantum cryptography and the security of its implementations.

My research focuses on **side-channel analysis** and **lattice-based cryptography**: masking,
leakage-resilient designs, and reductions from physical learning problems to standard ones.
I also work on hybrid homomorphic encryption and threshold signatures.

I received my PhD from [UCLouvain](https://www.uclouvain.be/) in 2025, under the supervision of
François-Xavier Standaert and Pierrick Méaux ([manuscript](https://hdl.handle.net/2078.5/242186)).
